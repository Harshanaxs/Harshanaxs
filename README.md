### What I do

- **Functional** — requirement gathering, process mapping and gap analysis across inventory, manufacturing, procurement, warehouse and accounting; translating how a business works into an ERP design, then UAT, training and go-live.
- **Technical** — Odoo customisation in Python (ORM, QWeb, custom modules), Directus back-office platforms, and the integrations, reports and data migrations an implementation really needs.
- **Full-stack build** — when a process needs more than the ERP: TypeScript services (NestJS / Node) behind Vue, Nuxt and Next.js, on PostgreSQL and Docker.
- **Domain** — hands-on with Sri Lankan and export SMEs (manufacturing, trading, warehousing), where the hard part is the process, not the code.
